## What happened

Both this repo's backend and frontend PH calendars have the wrong 2027 Hari Raya dates:

- `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/phRoster.ts`, `PH_YEAR_SLOTS[2027]` (~line 513, 516-517):
  Hari Raya Puasa on **2027-03-09**, Hari Raya Haji on **2027-05-16** (+ an In-Lieu row on
  2027-05-17, on the assumption 05-16 falls on a Sunday).
- `artifacts/roster-dashboard/src/lib/usePHActuals.ts`'s `SG_PH_META` — same wrong dates,
  independently duplicated on the frontend.

Replit's current code has corrected these to Hari Raya Puasa **2027-03-10** and Hari Raya Haji
**2027-05-17**, with no In-Lieu row (05-17 is not a Sunday). Replit also shipped a one-time
migration route (`POST /ph-roster-ref/sync-mom-calendar`) that moves any already-generated
roster rows and Master overrides from the old dates to the corrected ones, preserving assigned
officers.
