## What happened

In `buildSummary()` (`artifacts/api-server/src/routes/rosterPlan.ts`), two issues in the
generated FIRB deployment text:

1. **Double-listing.** This repo has no equivalent of Replit's `globalNamedCoverSet`/
   `globallyConsumedFromQueue` guards, so an officer who is both an explicit named cover for one
   unit (`coveredByOfficerName`) and a cross-post destination for another (`crossPostedToUnit`)
   can appear twice in the deployment text. Replit's own code comment gives a concrete repro:
   an officer covers unit A via `coveredByOfficerName`, but a different unit's
   `crossPostedToUnit` also points at them — without a dedup filter they show in both.
2. **REST vs OFF not distinguished.** This repo dumps both into one "OFF" list; Replit splits
   them into separate OFF and REST sections. A real content difference in an externally-shared
   summary, not just cosmetic.

## Fix

Port the dedup guard (track officers already placed via an explicit named cover before assigning
them to a cross-post slot) and split the OFF/REST output sections.
