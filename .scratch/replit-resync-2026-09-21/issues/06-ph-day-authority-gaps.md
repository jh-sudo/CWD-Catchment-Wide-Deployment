> *This was generated by AI during triage. Several related gaps sharing one root cause: nothing
> outside `PHHoliday.tsx` itself treats a saved PH/OIL roster as authoritative for that date.*

## What happened

**1. `buildSummary()` has no PH-awareness** (`rosterPlan.ts`). Replit's version makes saved
`ph-roster-ref` rows authoritative for PH dates and excludes PH participants from the OFF/LEAVE
lines (a `phActualNames` filter) so they aren't listed as both on-leave and deployed. This repo's
`buildSummary()` has neither.

**2. PH/OIL duty matched by name string, not unit code** (`TodaysRoster.tsx`, `RosterCycle.tsx`).
Both match officers to PH duty via `row.actualName || row.scheduledName` vs `o.name`
(case/trim-only). A typo'd or differently-cased name silently drops that officer from the PH-day
view entirely, and every non-matched officer is dropped from view rather than shown with their
normal duty. Replit matches by `o.unitCode === row.subCatchment` (stable/structural) with an
explicit OFF→ND exception fallback, and preserves normal duty data for officers not in a
PH-duty unit instead of blanking them. Replit's `TodaysRoster.tsx` also independently derives the
OIL-Monday roster by fetching the preceding Sunday's actual schedule
(`/roster-plan/schedule?date=oilOriginalDate`) rather than relying solely on the (separately
tracked, still-open) extract-Sunday flow — worth considering alongside the existing open ticket
`.scratch/roster-qa/issues/04` (OIL Monday silent-extract-failure).

**3. Non-override leave sources aren't cleared on a PH day** (`rosterPlan.ts`'s
`overrides/bulk` and `POST /roster-plan/ph-apply/:date`). Both only delete leave rows with
`source = 'override'`. An ordinary VL/SL applied via `POST /roster-plan/leave` *before* a date
became a configured PH/OIL day is never cleared, so that officer can show up simultaneously in
the PH-duty section and the LEAVE section of the FIRB summary/grid. Replit's `applyPHRoster`
additionally clears `leaveType === 'OIL'` rows and any PH-roster participant's leave row
regardless of source.

**4. No reverse-sync from Master-grid edits back to `ph_roster_ref`.** A manual correction made
directly in the Excel/Master grid for a PH date never propagates back to the PH Roster page, so
the two views can silently disagree after a manual fix. Replit added
`syncMasterEditsToPHRoster()` for this (with deliberately-scoped exclusions documented inline in
its `rosterPlan.ts`).

## Fix

Port each of the four behaviors above. They're independent enough to land as separate commits
even though they're bundled in one ticket (shared root cause / shared files).
