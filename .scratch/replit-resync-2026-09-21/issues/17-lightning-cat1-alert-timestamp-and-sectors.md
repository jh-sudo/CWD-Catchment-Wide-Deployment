## What happened

In `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/lightning.ts` / `lightning-monitor.ts`: the CAT1 push/alert
body embeds the raw `catEndOn` ISO timestamp verbatim (e.g. "until 2026-08-04T12:00:00.000Z")
instead of a human-readable time, and when multiple sectors are active it only ever uses the
*first* sector's end time for all of them.

## Fix

Port `formatCat1End()` (e.g. renders "4 Aug 2026 until 1200hrs") and report each active sector's
own end time rather than reusing the first sector's for all.

Not included — flagged as a UX/feature decision in `map.md` item 11, not a bug fix: per-sector
CAT1 subscription selection on the public `/lightning` page, OSM tiles instead of CARTO, and
