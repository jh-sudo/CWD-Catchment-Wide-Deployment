## What happened

**Frontend** (`manager.ts`'s `runRainAnalysis()`/`renderRainModal()`/`confirmRainAssign()`): the
current "Rain-Path Assign" button is always clickable regardless of data quality. `hasRain` is
computed from a blended location score (`current + 1.5*trend + 2*incoming`) that conflates "is
there rain" with "will a specific location be hit," and the average movement vector is always
trusted even when frame-to-frame vectors are wildly inconsistent (radar noise).

**Backend** (`deployments.ts`'s `rain-auto-assign`): fetches NEA station rainfall server-side and
assigns by crude region/cluster proximity, ignoring actual crew GPS.

Replit's rewrite separates `hasRain` (an actual radar blob, `centroids[0].w > 1`) from
`hasProjectedHits` (will it hit a scored location in 15-30 min), adds a `motionReliable` check
requiring at least 2 consistent movement vectors, and **disables** the assign button ("Movement
uncertain") when direction can't be trusted rather than letting a manager act on noise. The
matching backend rewrite takes client-projected `locationScores`/`projectedHitMinutes` and
greedily matches the *nearest* fresh-GPS crew (10-minute freshness gate), reporting
`skippedNoGps`.

## Fix

Port both halves together: the `hasRain`/`hasProjectedHits`/`motionReliable` split and button-gate
in `manager.ts`, and the nearest-fresh-GPS-crew matching algorithm in `deployments.ts`'s
`rain-auto-assign`, replacing the old NEA-station/cluster-proximity approach.

Related, smaller, optional: Replit's `optimize-assign` "nearest selected" mode can also reassign
already-deployed teams to a closer location (not just fill unassigned ones) — see `map.md` item
13 if picking this up too while in this code.
