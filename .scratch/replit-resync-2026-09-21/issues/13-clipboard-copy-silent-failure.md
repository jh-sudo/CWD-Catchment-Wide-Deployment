## What happened

Several "copy summary" buttons call `navigator.clipboard.writeText()` directly with no error
handling — `TodaysRoster.tsx`'s copy-deployment-summary button and `Layout.tsx`'s `handleCopy`.
If the call throws (common without a user gesture, on non-HTTPS, or in embedded/older mobile
browser contexts), the click silently does nothing with no feedback to the user.

## Fix

Wrap clipboard writes in a shared helper (Replit's `copyTextToClipboard`) that checks success and
surfaces a visible failure message/toast when it throws, instead of failing silently. Apply
everywhere `navigator.clipboard.writeText` is called directly in these two files.
