## What happened

**1. Primary/actual duty display is inverted.** Current logic:
`primaryDuty = targetDuty ?? actualDuty` — the calendar's big colored badge shows the officer's
*originally scheduled* duty, with any leave/override relegated to a small secondary tag that's
never colored as leave. An officer on approved MC/leave sees their normal working duty (e.g.
"ND") prominently displayed, with the leave status easy to miss.

**2. Partner-of-the-day resolution is incomplete**, in `CrewSchedule.tsx`, `MySchedule.tsx`, and
`Layout.tsx`'s "Crew Today" top-bar widget (same bug, three places): only looks at the officer's
static home-unit partner. Missing:
- When the *selected* officer is the one cross-posted elsewhere, it should look for a partner at
  the destination unit, not the home unit.
- When the normal partner is on leave and someone was explicitly named as their
  `coveredByOfficerName`, that covering officer should show as the day's partner instead of "no
  partner."

## Fix

1. Flip to `primaryDuty = actualDuty ?? targetDuty`, and color the primary badge as leave when
   applicable. Apply to both `CrewSchedule.tsx` and `MySchedule.tsx`.
2. Add the cross-post-destination and covered-by-name fallback cases to partner resolution.
   Apply to `CrewSchedule.tsx`, `MySchedule.tsx`, and `Layout.tsx`'s widget.
