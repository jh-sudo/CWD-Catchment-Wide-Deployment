Found during `/roster` QA (2026-09-08), while explaining how the PH roster
generate/implement flow works and checking whether its two independent
OIL-copy mechanisms could ever disagree. Same code as the deployed GOV PaaS
`api-server` — applies there too. **Higher severity than the other findings
in this set** — not a UX gap, actual wrong duty assignments on a real
holiday, hiding behind the default year of a commonly-used action.

## What was wrong

Two independently hand-maintained PH calendars exist in this codebase:
- `PH_YEAR_SLOTS` in `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/phRoster.ts` — drives
  `POST /api/ph-roster-ref/auto-allocate`, the engine that actually generates
  the PH duty roster.
- `SG_PH` in `artifacts/roster-dashboard/src/pages/PHHoliday.tsx` — drives
  the PH date picker and the `isOIL`/OIL-Monday detection shown to users.

For 2026 and 2028 these agree exactly. For **2027's Chinese New Year, they
didn't**:

| | Backend (`PH_YEAR_SLOTS`) — wrong | Frontend (`SG_PH`) — correct |
|---|---|---|
| CNY Day 1 | 2027-01-29 | 2027-02-06 |
| CNY Day 2 | 2027-01-30 | 2027-02-07 |
| CNY Day 2 (In Lieu) | *(didn't exist)* | 2027-02-08, copies from 02-07 |

Confirmed direction of the error via the lunar-calendar shift: 2026 CNY
(17 Feb, correct in both) minus the usual ~11-12 day yearly shift lands right
at 5-6 Feb 2027, matching the frontend's dates — the backend's Jan 29-30
looks like a real data-entry error, not an intentional divergence.

## Why this actually mattered, not just theoretically

`auto-allocate`'s default `targetYear` is **2027** — the fallback if no year
is passed in the request. Generating 2027 with the old table produced a real
PH roster on 29-30 Jan (dates that aren't Chinese New Year at all) and
silently generated nothing for 6-8 Feb — the dates the frontend's own picker
shows as CNY. A manager opening the PH page and selecting "CNY Day 1" (shown
as 6 Feb) would find nothing there.

Confirmed via direct DB query: this local dev DB already had stale
`ph_roster_ref` rows on 2027-01-29/01-30 from an earlier test run in this
same QA session, before the fix.

## Fix applied

Corrected `PH_YEAR_SLOTS`'s 2027 entries in `phRoster.ts` to match `SG_PH`'s
dates (6/7/8 Feb), including adding the missing In Lieu slot. Updated the
block's own comment, which previously (incorrectly) asserted "No other 2027
PHs fall on Sunday."

**Follow-up needed, not part of this fix**: no manual DB cleanup was done —
`auto-allocate` always wipes and replaces the *entire* target year on every
run, so simply re-running "Generate 2027 PH roster" clears the stale
Jan 29/30 rows and regenerates the whole year correctly. Whoever owns the
real cutover should re-run this for any environment where 2027 was already
generated under the old table (this local dev DB, and possibly GOV PaaS if
anyone ran it there).

## Broader risk not fixed here

The two calendars remain two independently-maintained sources of truth with
nothing checking they agree. This specific divergence is fixed, but the
*mechanism* that let it happen unnoticed is still there — a future year
added to one file and not the other would fail silently the same way, most
likely years further out that haven't been carefully cross-checked yet.
Worth a decision later on whether to derive one calendar from the other, or
add a startup/CI check that diffs them.
